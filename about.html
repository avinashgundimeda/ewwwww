About Nexora Technologies

A team of engineers, designers and mentors building dependable software and growing new talent.

Our story

Nexora Technologies started in 2014 as a three-person web studio in Hyderabad. Today we are a team of 60 engineers, designers and security specialists delivering software for clients in education, retail, healthcare and finance.

In 2018 we opened our internship programme after noticing that many graduates had theory but little project experience. Over 1,800 students have since trained with us, and many now work at our client companies.

Mission

Deliver reliable, secure software that helps organisations grow, and give students the practical experience to start strong careers.

Vision

To be the most trusted software partner and technical mentor for growing businesses and learners across India.
